Our Story

Holy Smokes is a high school BBQ team built on craftsmanship, teamwork, and service. Founded in 2026 in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania, we are starting by selling smoked food at the DC concession stand for exclusive school events.

Every plate is an opportunity to learn, every event is a chance to serve our school community, and every teammate is part of something bigger than themselves.
